Privacy policy

Slate · Effective 22 November 2026

Slate keeps everything on your tablet. It has no account and no analytics; the only thing that leaves the device is a city name or location for the weather widget, if you add one.

What the app uses

  • Your installed apps — to show, search and open them.
  • App usage history (with Usage access) — which apps you used recently and for how long, for the dock and the Screen time widget. It never leaves the device.
  • App names on the system Recents screen — read by the gesture service so the dock only shows apps that are still open. Nothing else on screen is read.
  • Calendar events (if you allow it) — shown in the Calendar and Up next widgets.
  • Contacts (if you allow it) — names and numbers in search results.
  • Photos you pick — shown in the Photo frame widget. Only the photo you choose is accessed.
  • What you write in widgets — notes, to-dos, habits and countdowns, stored on the device.

Permissions

  • Accessibility service (Slate gestures) — turns swipes at the screen edges into Home, Back and Recents, and reads the app names on the Recents screen (including the “Clear all” button) to keep the dock accurate. It does not read other screens or anything you type.
  • Usage access — recent apps and today's screen time.
  • Calendar, Contacts — optional, only for the widgets and search that use them.
  • Notifications — to alert you when a focus timer ends.
  • Internet — only for the weather widget.
  • Request uninstall — opens Android's uninstall screen when you choose Uninstall.

What we collect

Nothing. Slate has no account, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporting. The developer receives no data from the app. Everything it saves (your layout, lists, notes and settings) stays in the app's private storage on your device and is deleted when you uninstall it.

Third parties

The weather widget uses Open-Meteo: the city name you search for and that city's coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to fetch the forecast. No account or identifier is sent. Open-Meteo's own terms apply. Nothing else is shared.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
